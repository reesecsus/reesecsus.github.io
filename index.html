The Game of Golf

Golf is one of the greatest sports you can learn to play. Unlike team sports, golf is a game of personal challenge. It is you against your personal best score. The basics are simple: use a club to hit a ball from the tee box into the hole in as few strokes as possible. But mastering your swing, short game, and course management takes years of practice and patience. It is also fun at all levels. Whether you're playing a casual 9-hole round with friends or competing in a local tournament, golf rewards focus and consistency like no other sport. To learn more about getting started, check out the PGA's website.
